## The project
The website capacitameritoinclusione.it focuses on providing educational opportunities and reducing financial barriers to learning. They offer scholarships for students attending the third year of high school in Italy. The site emphasizes values of merit, inclusion, and equal access to quality education, referencing Article 34 of the Italian Constitution which concerns the right to education.
